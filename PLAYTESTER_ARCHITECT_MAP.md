## 5. Local crash recovery
localStorage keys: game `upkeep:playtest:v2:<userId>:<deckId>` (envelope: version, savedAt, fingerprint, linked session id + its last-seen updated_at, snapshot); index `upkeep:playtest:index:<userId>` (max 5 decks, LRU eviction); prefs `upkeep:playtest:prefs:v1:<userId>`. page.tsx passes userId. Read storage only after mount (no hydration mismatch). Write 1s after last change and at most every 5s while changing; also immediately on visibilitychange hidden and pagehide. Catch quota/blocked/parse errors, never throw; on failure disable recovery for the session and show banner "Crash recovery is off in this browser — save to your account to keep this game." A stored game failing validation or belonging to another deck is deleted with a notice. Sign-out: sign-out form in src/components/AppNav.tsx clears every `upkeep:playtest:` key before submitting; ALSO on load the play page deletes any `upkeep:playtest:*` keys belonging to another user id (covers expiry/other tabs/delete-account). Two tabs: last writer wins (document it).
