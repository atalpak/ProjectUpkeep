What you can do with Upkeep

Collection · Decks · Your playgroup
01 / Collection

Track the exact copy

Record printing, finish, language, quantity, and physical location. A foil and a nonfoil don’t get mixed together.

02 / Locations

Find it in your binder or box

Search for a card and see where every copy lives, including the ones sleeved in a deck.

03 / Availability

Know which copies are free

See the copies available to use separately from cards already committed to decks.

04 / Decklists

Check a list before building it

Paste a decklist to see what you own, what’s in another deck, and what you still need. Save it only if you want to.

05 / Decks

Keep track of what’s sleeved

Compare your decklist with the physical copies in its location. See what’s ready and what still needs a card.

06 / Wish list

Remember what you’re looking for

Keep the cards you want in one list and connect them to the decks you need them for.

07 / Friends

Find cards in your playgroup

See which friends have tradable copies of cards on your wish list. Search your collection and your circle.

08 / Trading

Keep both sides of a trade recorded

Propose a trade with a friend. When they accept, ownership updates in both collections and the trade stays in your history.

09 / Import

Bring an existing list with you

Import a CSV collection export or a card list instead of entering every card individually.

10 / iPhone scanning

Add cards from your phone

Scan a physical card with the iPhone app, confirm its printing, and choose where the copy belongs.

11 / Playtesting

Try your deck before game night

Explore opening hands and mulligans, or use the tabletop playtester to try out your deck.

12 / Prices & export

See estimates. Keep your data.

View Scryfall price estimates by finish. Export your collection as a CSV or card list whenever you need it.
